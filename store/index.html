Our Story

Made slowly, meant to last

Ochre began with a single clay vessel and a question: what if the things we live with were made with the same patience we wish we had? Every piece in the collection comes from a small studio. Each one is shaped, woven, cast, carved or blown by hand.

We make small runs and restock only when our makers are ready. When something sells out, it may be gone for a season.
